# Storylines

Three different films for the Printable Games Generator launch. **A was picked.**

**The one thing:** a teacher can get a complete, print-ready classroom game —
sheet, instructions and answer key — without an account, a server or a
connection.

**The one thing to remember:** it writes the answer key too, and it works with
the wifi off.

**The spine:** one teacher needs a Grade 4 maths word search for tomorrow. The
piece of real data that travels from hook to payoff is that one sheet —
`Maths Word Search`, Grade 4, Medium, 12×12, 3 pages, seed `jasper-coral-8177`.

---

## A · Period Three — *picked*

**Device:** a moment in someone's day. **Form:** object-to-object chain — every
shot ends on the thing that opens the next.

A teacher's sticky note becomes a blank sheet, the blank sheet becomes the app,
and the app's pages become paper on the desk. The viewer follows a person with a
deadline rather than a feature list.

- **First two seconds:** a yellow sticky note on warm paper, reading
  "Gr 4 · maths", and under it "Thurs · period 3". It settles, then lifts away.
- **Beats:** note → 11:42 pm and a blank sheet → the sheet becomes the app →
  search, pick, it builds, the answer key → three pages fan onto the desk →
  claim → end card.
- **Look:** the product's own ground (#EFEDE8 warm paper with its 22 px dot
  grid), ink #16140F, indigo #4F46E5 rationed to the selected card, the save
  button and the mark. Inter throughout, the app's own face.
- **Sound:** silent (see SPEC) — no music source is available in this
  environment. Intended: a calm room tone with a slow marimba pulse, paper and
  click sounds only on the real actions.

## B · Unplugged

**Device:** the feature as the hook. **Form:** one continuous take.

The grid fills itself while the connection indicator drops to offline, and the
camera never cuts — it pushes from the browser into the sheet and back out.

- **First two seconds:** letters landing into the word-search grid, the
  connection indicator going dark in the corner.
- **Beats:** it is working offline → the spinner everything else gives you →
  reveal → grade, theme, regenerate, all offline → the PDF saves → claim → end
  card.
- **Look:** the app's dark theme as the ground, the white sheet the only light.
- **Sound:** a room hum that cuts at the unplug, then only mechanical clicks.

## C · 37,800

**Device:** a number lands. **Form:** one frame that rebuilds.

One arithmetic problem is verified on screen, then multiplies into the 37,800
the test suite re-derives on every run.

- **First two seconds:** `6 × 7 = 42` with a verification tick, then it
  multiplies across the frame.
- **Beats:** one problem proved → what a wrong answer key costs in front of a
  class → reveal → the count climbing, then the sheet it protects → the answer
  key → claim → end card.
- **Look:** graph paper, very large numerals, accent only on the tick.
- **Sound:** a tick per verification building into a swell.

Strongest for an engineering audience, weakest at showing what the product is.
